PulseWay — Live Demo & Interactive Walkthrough
1. Quick Access
Open the live deployment URL and enable Dual Split-View in the top navigation bar to run the Citizen Mobile App (Left) and the Hospital Command Center (Right) side-by-side with real-time sync.

2. Interactive Feature Flows
Flow A: Citizen SOS & 3-Stage Legal Verification
Profile Setup (Left Window): Sign in or register with patient details (Name, Age, Blood Group, Medical Conditions, Emergency Contacts).

AI Triage & Emergency Trigger:

Pick an emergency from the 6 clinical presets (e.g., Severe Chest Pain / Cardiac, Major Road Accident, Brain Stroke) or type/speak symptoms in English, Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), or Hindi (हिन्दी).

Click Call Ambulance (108).

3-Stage Dispatch Verification:

Stage 1 (Patient Selection): Choose "Myself" (attaches registered medical profile) or "Someone Else" (prompts bystander to estimate age, gender, height, and blood group).

Stage 2 (Dispatch Confirmation): Confirm the ambulance request.

Stage 3 (Anti-Prank Warning): Acknowledge the statutory deterrent under the BNS & IT Act against false emergency filings.

Dynamic Escalation: The citizen app enters an active search state. If unacknowledged, the search radius automatically expands by +5 km every 60 seconds.

Flow B: Hospital Command Dispatch & Bed Lifecycle
Hospital Login (Right Window): Select a hospital preset (Manipal Super Specialty, Victoria Govt. Hospital, or Apollo Hospital) to inspect live ambulance fleet, ICU beds, and trauma bay telemetry.

Accept & Allot:

View the incoming SOS card with live GPS coordinates, critical triage status, clinical vitals, and a direct patient call trigger.

Click "Allot Ambulance Now".

Citizen Telemetry Transition (Left Window):

The citizen screen immediately flips to live ambulance tracking and plays an automated voice reassurance announcement.

Displays ambulance registration, driver details, live ETA countdown, and one-tap WhatsApp/SMS family alerts.

Bed Allocation & Discharge:

Hospital Window: Click "Allot ER / ICU Bed" to assign a specific slot (e.g., ICU-BED-04), immediately decrementing the live available bed counter.

Discharge: Click "Confirm Treatment Done & Discharge" (or citizen clicks "Ambulance Has Reached — Return to Home Page").

The bed automatically returns to the hospital's available pool, and the incident archives permanently to the citizen's AI Chat & Incident History (accessible via the top-left menu alongside the Offline Survival Guide).

Flow C: Solo Commute Crash Detector Demo
Click "Trigger Crash (0 km/h)" inside the Accident Demo Lab on the top bar.

Simulates an emergency vehicular impact: rapid deceleration from 42 km/h to 0 km/h with a 6.4G impact spike, initiating a high-priority 60-second countdown.

Auto-SOS Trigger: If the commuter does not press "I Am Safe — Cancel Alert" within 60 seconds, PulseWay automatically dispatches an emergency SOS with exact GPS coordinates and medical history directly to the nearest hospital command desk.
